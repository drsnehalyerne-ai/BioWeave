Normal (n=45)

## Major Findings

## Working Biological Model

Microbial challenge may contribute to epithelial barrier disruption and extracellular matrix remodeling during early oral malignant transformation.

Proposed sequence:

Microbial stimulation
→ Bacteria-responsive host signaling
→ Cell adhesion disruption
→ ECM remodeling
→ Dysplastic transformation
