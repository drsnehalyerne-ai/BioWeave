# Bridge Gene Hypothesis v1

## Purpose

To identify candidate bridge genes that may connect host defense activation, extracellular matrix remodeling, and epithelial barrier disruption during the transition from normal oral mucosa to oral dysplasia.

---

## Concept of Bridge Genes

Bridge genes are highly connected network components that link distinct biological modules. In the current BioWeave analysis, three major modules emerged:

### Module 1: Host Defense and Immune Activation

Representative genes:

* STAT1
* ISG15
* MX1
* OAS2
* DDX58
* CCL2

### Module 2: Extracellular Matrix Remodeling

Representative genes:

* FN1
* COL1A1
* COL1A2
* COL3A1
* COL4A1
* COL4A2
* MMP2
* MMP9

### Module 3: Epithelial Adhesion and Barrier Integrity

Representative genes:

* CD44
* CLDN10
* Junction-associated genes
* Cadherin-associated genes

---

## Candidate Bridge Gene 1: FN1

### Biological Role

Fibronectin 1 (FN1) is a major extracellular matrix glycoprotein involved in:

* Cell adhesion
* Cell migration
* Tissue remodeling
* Integrin signaling

### Network Observation

FN1 demonstrated the highest degree in the STRING network, suggesting a central role in coordinating extracellular matrix reorganization.

### Hypothesis

FN1 may function as a central remodeling hub linking inflammatory signaling to structural changes in the dysplastic epithelium.

---

## Candidate Bridge Gene 2: TGFB1

### Biological Role

Transforming Growth Factor Beta 1 (TGFB1) regulates:

* Fibrosis
* Extracellular matrix deposition
* Immune regulation
* Epithelial plasticity

### Network Observation

TGFB1 occupied a highly connected position within the interaction network.

### Hypothesis

TGFB1 may represent a key mediator connecting host defense responses to extracellular matrix remodeling.

---

## Candidate Bridge Gene 3: STAT1

### Biological Role

STAT1 is a major transcription factor controlling:

* Interferon signaling
* Antiviral responses
* Innate immune activation

### Network Observation

STAT1 emerged as a prominent immune-related hub.

### Hypothesis

STAT1 may initiate host defense programs that subsequently influence tissue remodeling pathways.

---

## Candidate Bridge Gene 4: CCL2

### Biological Role

CCL2 is involved in:

* Monocyte recruitment
* Chemokine signaling
* Immune-stromal communication

### Network Observation

CCL2 connects immune-related pathways with tissue remodeling processes.

### Hypothesis

CCL2 may facilitate communication between inflammatory and structural compartments during dysplasia development.

---

## Candidate Bridge Gene 5: CD44

### Biological Role

CD44 participates in:

* Cell adhesion
* Cell migration
* Extracellular matrix interactions
* Stemness-associated signaling

### Network Observation

CD44 occupies an interface between adhesion-related and extracellular matrix-related networks.

### Hypothesis

CD44 may contribute to epithelial reorganization and altered cell-matrix interactions during early transformation.
